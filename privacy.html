Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Trap Tracker respects your privacy. This app is designed to function without collecting or sharing personal data.

What Data We Collect

Trap Tracker does not collect, track, or transmit personal information.

  • No user accounts
  • No analytics
  • No advertising trackers
  • No third-party data sharing

Location Data

Location data is used only when you choose to use GPS-based features, such as placing a trap using a photo's embedded location.

Location information:

  • Is processed locally on your device
  • Is never sold or shared
  • Is never guessed or inferred

Photos

Photos you take or upload are stored locally on your device and used only to help you visually identify trap locations.

Photos are never uploaded, analyzed by third parties, or shared.

Offline Use & Sync

Trap Tracker works offline. Data remains on your device and syncs only when connectivity is available.

Your Control

You are always in control of your data:

  • GPS is never guessed—it comes only from photo metadata or manual placement
  • Manual placements are never overridden without your confirmation
  • All trap data can be deleted at any time
