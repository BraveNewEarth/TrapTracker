Key Features

Built for the Field

Professional tools that work without cellular service, designed for real-world pest control.

Photo-First Workflow

Take a photo in the field. If your camera has location enabled, Trap Tracker automatically places the trap using the photo's GPS data. No typing coordinates.

GPS Mapping

Every trap appears on an interactive map with satellite imagery. Tap any marker to see details, log catches, or get directions. Never wonder where you put that trap again.

Offline-First Design

Works without cellular service. Your data syncs automatically when a connection is available. Perfect for rural properties, vineyards, and remote land.

Color-Coded Status

Green means good. Amber means due soon. Red means overdue or needs attention. See what needs checking at a glance without reading every detail.

How It Works

Simple by Design

Get started in under a minute. No account required.

1

Take a Photo

When you place a trap, take a photo. If your camera has location enabled, Trap Tracker automatically extracts the GPS coordinates and places the trap on your map.

💡 Enable Precise Location for best results
2

Or Place Manually

If a photo doesn't include GPS—or if you want to place traps ahead of time—long-press anywhere on the map to drop a pin. Adjust if needed, save, and add a photo later.

3

Check & Update

When you check a trap, log the result: Good, Caught, Sprung, or Maintenance. The schedule resets automatically, and you'll always know what needs attention next.
